About the Department

The Department of Computer Science offers undergraduate and graduate programs built on strong fundamentals, hands-on learning, and industry-aligned research. Our faculty guide students through modern software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and data science.

Mission

Inspire students to innovate and solve real-world problems using computing.

Vision

Become a leading center for computer science education and research.

Values

Integrity, collaboration, excellence, and lifelong learning.
